What makes it special

Cinnamon Bay is the longest beach on St. John and has a wider, more open feel. It pairs well with a beach day, a campground stop, water activities, and a quick look at historic ruins nearby.

Longest beachWater sportsCampgroundHistoric ruins nearby
Best conditions

Best in light to moderate wind. It can be beautiful when conditions are calm, but it may feel breezier than Maho or Francis during stronger east or northeast winds.

Helpful tips
  • Great for walking the beach.
  • Good choice when you want space.
  • Can feel more exposed when wind picks up.
