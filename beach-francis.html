What makes it special

Francis Bay sits near the end of the North Shore Road and is often calmer and quieter than the island’s headline beaches. It is a good fit when you want a slower beach day.

QuietLong swimsBirding nearbyProtected option
Best conditions

Best when you want a protected North Shore option, especially in moderate to strong east or northeast wind.

Helpful tips
  • Good choice when Maho feels too busy.
  • Nearby Francis Bay walking trail is a nice add-on.
  • Bring what you need since services are limited.
