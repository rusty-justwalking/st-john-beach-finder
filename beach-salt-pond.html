What makes it special

Salt Pond Bay is a South Shore beach with clear water and a more rugged feel. It’s a great option when north winds impact the North Shore, and it’s also the starting point for the Ram Head hike.

⚠️ Local Tips
  • There is a short but rocky hike down to the beach from the parking area.
  • Limited shade, so bring sun protection.
  • Water is often clear, but conditions can change quickly with wind.
  • This is the starting point for the Ram Head Trail, so it can get busier in the morning.

Best conditions

Best when winds are from the north or northwest, making the South Shore the better side of the island. Can be hot and exposed.

Helpful tips
  • Bring water, especially if hiking Ram Head.
  • The walk back to parking is uphill.
  • Avoid hanging around late afternoon if bugs are active.
