What makes it special

Trunk Bay is the iconic St. John beach, famous for its overlook view, soft sand, and underwater snorkeling trail. It is also one of the most popular and accessible beaches on the island.

Best conditions

Best when winds are light to moderate from the east, northeast, south, southeast, or southwest. It can get busier and more exposed than Maho in stronger wind.

Helpful tips
  • This is the beach most visitors want to see.
  • Expect crowds when cruise traffic is high.
  • There is typically an entrance fee, so bring a card or cash backup.
