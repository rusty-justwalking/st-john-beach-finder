What makes it special

Hawksnest is a local favorite because it is close to Cruz Bay, easy to reach, and gives you a classic St. John beach day without making the drive too long. It has reef patches near shore and a more intimate feel than the larger beaches.

Close to Cruz BaySnorkelingLocal favoriteNorth Shore
Best conditions

Best when winds are light to moderate from the east, northeast, south, southeast, or southwest. It can also be a solid choice when South Shore winds make the North Shore calmer.

Helpful tips
  • Arrive early for easier parking.
  • Good first beach stop after picking up a Jeep.
  • Reef patches can be good for snorkeling when the water is settled.
