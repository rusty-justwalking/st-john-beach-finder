What makes it special

Honeymoon Beach has a lively beach-club feel compared with some of the quieter National Park beaches. It is close to Cruz Bay by trail or boat, with rentals and services often available nearby.

Beach club feelRentals nearbyHike or boat accessCaneel Bay area
Best conditions

Best on lighter wind days or when North Shore conditions are generally calm. It is less of a protected-wind fallback than Maho.

Helpful tips
  • Good if you want a beach day with more energy.
  • Can be reached by Lind Point Trail from Cruz Bay.
  • Check current access and services before relying on rentals or food.
