Tektite Trail

South Shore hike off Lameshur Road. The National Park Service notes the trailhead is on the right side of Lameshur Road at the first switchback.

ModerateApprox. 1.6 miles round trip1 to 1.5 hours
Starting areaLameshur Road, right side at the first switchback
Best time to goMorning or late afternoon when the South Shore sun is less intense.
NearbyGreat Lameshur, Little Lameshur, Yawzi Point

Why hike it

  • Trailhead off Lameshur Road near the first switchback
  • Historic Tektite research area
  • Dry forest and coastal terrain
  • Less crowded than North Shore hikes

Trail tips

  • Bring plenty of water. This area can feel hot and exposed.
  • Check road conditions and rental car rules before driving toward Lameshur.
Bring water, sun protection, and shoes with grip. Avoid starting exposed hikes late in the day, and leave enough daylight to return safely.
