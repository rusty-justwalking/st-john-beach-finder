What makes it special

Waterlemon Cay and Leinster Bay are better thought of as a snorkeling adventure than a simple beach stop. You walk the Leinster Bay Trail and then swim out to the cay when conditions are safe.

SnorkelingAdventure stopLeinster Bay TrailMarine life
Best conditions

Best on calm days with low wind and good visibility. Avoid when winds or currents make the swim questionable.

Helpful tips
  • This is not a simple park-and-plop beach.
  • Only swim to the cay if conditions are safe and you are confident.
  • Bring water, sun protection, and secure snorkel gear.
