Cruz Bay Overlook

Short overlook option above Cruz Bay with views toward town, St. Thomas, and the harbor.

Easy to ModerateApprox. 0.5 to 1.0 mile depending start20 to 45 minutes
Starting areaCruz Bay / Lind Point area
Best time to goLate afternoon or golden hour, with a headlamp if returning near dark.
NearbyCruz Bay, Lind Point, Honeymoon Beach

Why hike it

  • Quick scenic viewpoint
  • Good near town
  • Nice sunset feel

Trail tips

  • Do not wait too late because bugs and darkness can sneak up fast.
  • Good add-on to Lind Point.
Bring water, sun protection, and shoes with grip. Avoid starting exposed hikes late in the day, and leave enough daylight to return safely.
